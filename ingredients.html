Five Vitamins, Five Short Guides

Gelagen names biotin, vitamin B12, vitamin B6, folate and pantothenic acid. Exact amounts live on your bottle.

1

Biotin (Vitamin B7)

A water-soluble B vitamin found in eggs, nuts and seeds, often linked with hair and nail care.

Related research →

2

Vitamin B12 (Methylcobalamin)

A B vitamin that comes mostly from animal foods, here in its methylcobalamin form.

Related research →

3

Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine HCl)

A B vitamin used in many everyday body processes, here as pyridoxine hydrochloride.

Related research →

4

Folate (Vitamin B9)

The B vitamin found in leafy greens and beans, also called vitamin B9.

Related research →

5

Pantothenic Acid (D-Calcium Pantothenate)

Vitamin B5, found in almost every food, here as the calcium salt of the d-form.
