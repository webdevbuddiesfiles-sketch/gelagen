Who Should Check With A Doctor Before Gelagen

Gelagen is meant for adults, and its guidance names specific groups who should check with a professional first. Here's a closer look.

If You're Pregnant Or Nursing

Anyone who is pregnant or nursing should consult a healthcare professional before starting Gelagen or any new dietary supplement.

If You're Under 18

Gelagen is meant for adults aged 18 and older; it is not intended for use by minors.

If You Take Medication

Anyone taking medication should check with a professional first, since vitamin supplements can sometimes interact with certain prescriptions.

If You Have A Medical Condition

Anyone with a known medical condition should review the full label — see our Ingredients page — with their healthcare provider before starting. Gelagen does not replace prescribed medication or medical care.
