Biotin and Pantothenic Acid: The Everyday B Vitamins

Two of Gelagen's five named vitamins — biotin and pantothenic acid — are both B vitamins found in everyday foods. Here's what's behind them.

Biotin (Vitamin B7)

Biotin is a water-soluble B vitamin found in eggs, nuts and seeds, often linked with hair and nail care in wellness formulas like Gelagen.

Pantothenic Acid (Vitamin B5)

Pantothenic acid, included in Gelagen as d-calcium pantothenate, is found in almost every food and plays a role in many everyday body processes.

Why They're Paired Together

Both are B vitamins with a long history of use in beauty-from-within formulas, which is why they sit alongside B12, B6 and folate in Gelagen's five-vitamin blend — see our companion piece on the other three.

Where To See Exact Amounts

The exact amount of each vitamin is on your jar's Supplement Facts panel. See the full ingredient breakdown for research links.
